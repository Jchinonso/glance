Welcome to Glance

Glance sits on top of your screen during calls and interviews. When you're asked a question, it shows you what to say.

It hears the question
Glance follows the call and picks out questions as they're asked.
You get an answer in seconds
Built from the conversation and whatever is on your screen, such as code or a shared doc.
It sounds like you
In Interview mode, answers come from your resume, in your words, ready to say aloud.

Setup takes about a minute, and you can change everything later in Settings.

About you

So answers sound like you. All optional; change it later in Settings > Interview.

You're set

  • When the call starts, press Listen (). macOS asks for screen and audio access the first time: allow it, then reopen Glance once, since macOS only sends audio after a reopen.
  • In Interview mode, Glance answers each question on its own once the interviewer finishes. Any time, you can press Answer () or type a question.
  • Everything here lives in Settings, the gear at the top.
New interview? Keep the same role, or add its job description.

    Custom instructions

    Added to Glance's built-in instructions on every question. Tell it how you like answers: tone, length, language, what to focus on, things to avoid. Longer instructions are compressed when you save, so each question sends fewer tokens.

    This looks like a resume. Paste it under Settings > Interview > Resume instead: custom instructions are compressed when saved, which rewrites wording; Resume is sent word for word.
